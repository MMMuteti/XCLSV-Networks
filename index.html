The studio

A product branding studio based in Machakos.

XCLSV works with founders and teams who make things — food, goods, hospitality, trades — and need those things to be recognisable. Machakos is home base. The work is meant to travel: market stalls, retail shelves, and screens.

Every identity is checked in three places before it is called finished: the stall, the shelf, and the phone.

  • Practical files. Artwork a local printer can run.
  • One system. Logo, pack, social, and merch share the same rules.
  • Room to grow. The next product should not need a new argument.
  • Close enough to visit. Workshops in Machakos, reviews wherever the team is.

Process

From a conversation to a launch kit.

Start with the product

We look at what you sell, the price, and the places it is bought. A stall and a supermarket ask different things of the same brand.

Place it in the market

Who it is for, what it should be called, and which neighbouring brands it must not resemble. Positioning comes before decoration.

Build the system

Logo, colour, type, and a short line. You see options on a label, a shutter, and a phone — not only on a white artboard.

Apply it

Packaging, social templates, print, and merch come from the same rules so a new teammate can extend the brand.

Hand over the kit

Guidelines, files, and a simple campaign sequence. You leave with something the team can run.
